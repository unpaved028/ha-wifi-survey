# WiFi Survey

Plan your WiFi with your own floor plans. Upload a PDF or an image, let the app detect the walls, place your router and repeaters and get coverage maps for 2.4 and 5 GHz on every floor and across floors. Move or add repeaters in a simulation, let the app suggest better spots, and find a misplaced phone room by room.

- Planning works with any router; measure with the Android app that comes with WiFi Survey.
- Full support for FRITZ!Box mesh networks (measurement runs, device finder) and Shelly devices.
- No plan at hand? The start page loads an example house to try everything.

The user interface is in German for now; English is on its way.

**Deutsch:** Grundrisse hochladen, Wände erkennen lassen, Router und Repeater setzen. Daraus entstehen Abdeckungskarten für 2,4 und 5 GHz, eine Simulation, Empfehlungen für bessere Plätze und eine Gerätesuche nach Räumen.
